About Us

We Create Furniture That Tells Your Story

At Md Wajid Sofa Works, we believe that furniture is more than just functional pieces – it's an expression of your personality and lifestyle. Located at Khaprail More, Matigara in Siliguri, West Bengal, we've been crafting premium L-shaped sofas, corner sofas, recliner sofas, and motor seats that blend traditional craftsmanship with modern design.

Our skilled team of artisans brings decades of experience to every piece we create. We specialize in all kinds of sofa sets and motor/car seats. From selecting the finest materials to the final stitch, we ensure every product meets our exacting standards.
